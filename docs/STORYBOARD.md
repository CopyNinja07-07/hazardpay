# Hazard Pay: the film (v2, 2:50)

**The one idea every viewer must leave with:** *we crash-test a delivery robot 120 times in a computer
before its job; how often it crashes is the insurance price; if it really crashes, we replay it to see why
and PayPal pays out automatically.*

Style: dark, quiet, precise, with long takes and slow camera moves. Wide shots establish the space; low-angle shots make
Bolt feel like a character; a held silent beat before every failure. The 3D is rendered live in the browser from the
real MuJoCo recordings (every robot motion on screen is real physics, not keyframed). Then short real-app proof clips.
Voice: Suryansh. Score: original orchestral cues (MusicGen), D major, then B minor for the adversary, then back.

## Who is Bolt
A six-wheel sidewalk delivery robot, 22 kg, about knee height, blue lid, orange flag so drivers see him.
Named **Bolt** because delivery robots are judged on one thing: *getting there*. He has two LED eyes so people know
which way he's going, and they show how he's doing: cyan for fine, amber for worried, red X for failed, green for
delivered.
